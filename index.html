Sustainable Development Goals

As a top consumer of diesel fuel for our trucks, we're committed to complying with EURO 4 engine emission requirements, capping CO at 1.0g/km, Total Hydro Carbons at 0.10g/km, and NOx at 0.08g/km.

We also partner with domestic and international shipping lines to source low-sulfuric bunker fuels, reflecting our ongoing commitment to sustainable operations.
