Our Services

Explore our end-to-end logistics solutions, from inter-island sea freight and nationwide trucking to warehousing, customs clearance, and shipping agency.

Sea Freight Forwarding

Dry and perishable goods shipments via container, backed by our large pool of partnered inter-island shipping lines.

Break Bulk

Cargo too large for standard containers, transported in bags, boxes, crates, drums, or rolled/lifted onto a vessel.

Container

20ft and 40ft dry containers, 18–25 ton capacity, handling everything from food grade to scraps.

Loose Cargo

Computed per CBM, based on dimension (L×W×H) and weight in kilograms.

Cold Chains

20ft and 40ft reefer containers, 18–25 ton capacity, food grade to scraps.

Trucking & Hauling Services

Pick-up and delivery through Prime Mover Tractor Trucks and Wingvans with nationwide scope, connecting the islands of the Philippines.

Prime Mover

To guarantee on it's top quality service, YM Cargo Transport Corp. has invested over 100 units of Brand New Prime Mover Tractor Trucks to boost its fleet nationwide. Compliant to EURO 4 emission requirement by the industry, we believe that the company is supporting its sustainability drive towards its future movements. Moreover, YM Cargo has produced over 200 units of Trailers for Containers since 2021 with its production facility in Manila City.

10 Wheeler Wingvan

To support the local movements of our clients, we have expanded our range of fleets with Wingvan type closed vans to cater immediate door to door transfers nationwide. The trucks will ply to any points in the Philippines via RORO movements.

Trailer

Our trailer comprises from 20 footer to 40 footer combo-type double & triple axles. It is locally produced in our Manila manufacturing facility.

Container Vans

We have more than 250 units of 20 footer container vans that is fit for food grade requirements. These owned units are specially catered to our beloved patron clients.

Cranes and Forklifts

Our heavy equipment is capable of handing Lift on-Lift off movements within our yard facilities.

Trucks

A wide pool of trucks from Prime Movers to Closed Vans with various weight capacity to choose from.
