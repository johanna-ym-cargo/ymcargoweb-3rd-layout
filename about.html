Our History

Celebrating it’s 20th Year of service, YM Cargo Transport Corporation is proud to announce that the corporation is now fully-owned by Businessman Edwin G. Reyes and his family. As the initials YM represent, Yen Gie Reyes-Armenion (Director) and Mark Anthony Reyes (Director) join their parents, Mr. Edwin G. Reyes (Chairman of the Board of Directors) and his wife Mrs. Mary Fe Reyes (Director), as stockholders of the corporation.

The YM Cargo Transport Corp. was established September 8, 2003 at Cebu City, Philippines. It had humble beginnings using only 3 units of Prime Mover trucks from the latter’s Metro Yen Motors, a Truck Surplus business owned by Mr. Edwin Reyes.

Mr. Reyes is a highly competitive businessman and a Customs Broker by profession. He steered the YM Cargo Transport Corp. company from a small scale Forwarding and Trucking business with the help of its pioneering employees.

Core Values

Safety Has No Compromise

Safety is imperative to our success and the future of our company, and we take personal responsibility for the safety of ourselves and those around us. We perform safety assessments as we continually improve protocol and procedures while providing our people with the training, knowledge and tools to work safely and prevent accidents.

Our Most Valuable Asset is Our Employees

While working as a team, we accomplish our goals by treating others with respect, compassion and integrity. We aim to attract, develop, and retain the best people by creating a stimulating, fun, and open work culture. #WeMoveasONE

We Can Make A Difference

We aim to make a positive difference to the communities where we operate and seek ways to assist them. We prioritize local hiring and work to minimize the impact of our activities on the environment.

We Value Our Customers

We are committed to providing the highest quality service in meeting our customer’s expectations. We focus on customer relationships that build trust, confidence, and loyalty.

Continuously Improve

The responsibility to push for greatness is placed upon everyone within the company. By instituting on-the-job training, management effectively instills knowledge and trust in their employees.

Lead with Financial Strength and Stability

We believe that efficiency and financial strength allow us to achieve our corporate goals and create opportunities. We manage financial risk systematically and responsibly.
